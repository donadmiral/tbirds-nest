# Member volume rollout, 6 November 2026

The capacity objective is five million daily active members. The production limit for simultaneous broadcasts is 10. The launch target is 1,000 broadcasts at once, including a 100,000-viewer event. The team has yet to qualify either objective with representative traffic.

## Five-million qualification

The checked-in base model assumes 30 active minutes, 200 application requests per member per day and a fivefold peak. It implies approximately 57,870 peak API requests/s and 442,709 simultaneous Realtime connections. These are planning inputs. The gate requires authenticated messages, feeds, writes, uploads, media playback and staff traffic simultaneously, recovery from failures, regional results, at least 24 hours at the target stage and approved provider capacity and spend.
