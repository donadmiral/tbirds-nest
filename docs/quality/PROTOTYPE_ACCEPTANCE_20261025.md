# Prototype acceptance: 25 October 2026

This release implements the owner's October 25 scope: defer required MFA for the single QA owner; preserve current music and defer end-to-end encryption, delivery-provider integration and expanded company staff access; strengthen verification, marketplace reviews, applications, sharing, stories and the shared palette. It is not a production scale or Instagram/Meta feature-parity certification.

## Implemented behavior to exercise

1. **Owner operations:** the existing QA owner can use a password-only active session when the explicit database policy is enabled. Every command still checks staff role and session, records its audit and retains idempotency. Optional authenticator setup remains in Account security. A second active staff member or loss of sole super-admin ownership permanently retires the exception until explicitly reconfigured. The frontend exception is limited to QA Supabase `uaulhuelcfkjwojtymmw`.
2. **Verification:** application evidence and a review basis are mandatory; client profile edits cannot confer a badge or forge a decision. Revocation updates the application and public identity together. Badges refresh through a bounded shared cache and retain exactly their existing graphics. Impersonation reports are private. Existing approvals are not retrospectively identity-certified by these checks.
3. **Marketplace:** only the buyer of a completed, committed order can submit that order's rating. Legacy ratings lacking purchase evidence remain stored but are excluded from public verified ratings. Reviews and listing reports have dedicated forms, errors and retry. Staff moderation is audited and replay-safe; report history survives account/review removal without preserving a broken foreign-key link. Delivery states remain infrastructure only; no courier partnership is activated.
4. **Applications:** web and phone load bounded, account-owned pages; failed requests display retry instead of an empty history. Candidates can withdraw with confirmation. Application/interview history stays intact, with one withdrawal event and an employer notification. Hiring notifications link to the correct history or recruiter desk. Company recruitment stays within its existing owner permissions.
5. **Sharing:** direct, group and community post shares use the durable outbox, one stable message ID, account checks and lost-response reconciliation. A failed or queued request is not shown as delivered. Errors in conversation loading are visible and retryable. A late share result cannot close another post's sheet.
6. **Stories:** trending reads recheck current visibility, expiry, blocking, muting and active profile state. Old ranking snapshots expire. Client previews discard unresolved stories and preserve the open viewing sequence during background refresh. The web canvas uses 9:16 coordinates, centre guides, draggable layers, editable text and readable backgrounds. Dual capture is explicitly sequential scene plus reaction photo, with readiness checks and lifecycle cancellation; it requests 1080p video where supported.
7. **Palette:** web and admin use the phone's white/navy/warm-neutral light palette and navy dark palette. System-theme preference follows route changes and other tabs, including safe behavior with unavailable storage. The admin rail collapses on small viewports, and header/menus avoid fixed-width overflow. Badge artwork and media-viewer contrast styling are preserved.

## Music extension boundary

Apple catalog metadata remains separate from reusable sound recordings. `shared/appleMusic.ts` discards provider preview/playback URLs, and the Apple edge endpoint does not grant soundtrack capability. Properly approved, pre-cut, first-party audio can be provisioned in `story_sounds` as a licensed library record and selected by the existing web/phone authoring path. Its storage ownership and content-media binding must agree.

A future restricted commercial catalog requires agreement-specific territory, expiry, business/advertising usage and durable license references; playback must recheck current rights even for previously published stories. Excerpt editing, soundtrack-rendered exports and ordinary post/carousel soundtracks are separate engineering work. A developer account or newly signed agreement does not automatically activate those features. No commercial music or E2EE claim is made in this release.

## Acceptance beyond automated checks

Use separate owner, ordinary member, verified applicant, seller, buyer and employer accounts. Check direct-route access as well as buttons. Exercise interrupted requests, duplicate taps, account switching, blocked users, revoked verification and removed story access. Test uploads and playback on actual iPhone/Android devices and supported desktop/tablet browsers, including low bandwidth, app backgrounding, device rotation, keyboard navigation and screen-reader labels.

Provider-backed calls, IntoBank/Crisp settlement and refunds need their actual QA credentials and controlled provider evidence. Real camera/audio performance and five-million-user capacity require device/load measurement. Inherited database advisories are tracked separately; passing this release's checks does not mean the entire database has zero advisory findings.
